When not doing research, you can find me playing [competitive Pokémon VGC](https://championships.pokemon.com/en-us/). I started playing official Pokémon tournaments in 2018 in California. Since then, I have qualified for five Pokémon World Championships (2019, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025). I finished as the 1st in the UK in the 2024 season and 3rd in the 2025 season on the [championship points leaderboard](https://www.pokemon.com/us/play-pokemon/leaderboards/vg-masters/). I have had three top-8 finishes in major tournaments with ~500 players. I am a member of Team China in the 2024 [Pokémon VGC World Cup](https://worldcupvgc.com/). I am also a founder of Nanjing Pokémon Monthly and the Jiangsu-Zhejiang-Shanghai Pokémon League, grassroots tournaments that provide opportunities for China's underrepresented Pokémon players to make friends, improve their skills, and become globally competitive.



My favorite Pokémon is [Togedemaru](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Togedemaru_(Pok%C3%A9mon)).
